# The $1 Website

Live pages: /1-dollar-website/ (15 designs, intake form) and /1-dollar-website/<design>/ (a five page preview of each).
Quote builder: a new "$1 Website" service under Websites & Development, or /package-builder/?template=<slug>.

## Terms as built (confirm before advertising)
- $1 setup. $99 a month hosting. 12 month minimum, so $1,189 for the first year.
- Up to 5 pages: Home, Services, About, Gallery, Contact. Layout stays as previewed. We fill in the customer's text, photos, logo and colors.
- Extra pages $150 each. Copywriting $120 per page. Domain fees go to the registrar.
- Cancelling before month 12: the remaining hosting months are due. After month 12 the customer can cancel in writing.
- Unpaid hosting: written notice, then the site comes down. The customer keeps their domain, text and photos. The design and code stay with Wavefront Studio.
- In the quote builder it stays out of the bundle count, the bundle discount, the first month free offer and every sale deal.
- The terms text on the page and in the FAQ is plain English written from these decisions. Have it checked by counsel before heavy advertising, especially the early cancellation and takedown wording.

## How a customer buys
1. Pick a design on /1-dollar-website/ (or preview it first).
2. Send the intake form: business details, services, hours, colors, a link to logo and photos. It arrives in the lead inbox as "$1 Website intake".
3. We reply within two working days, invoice the $1, and confirm a go-live date. There is no card checkout on the site for this yet.
