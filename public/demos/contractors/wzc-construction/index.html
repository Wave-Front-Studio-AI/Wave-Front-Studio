Before you hire

Have a question that isn't here? Call Walter at (213) 841-0729.

Is WZC a licensed contractor?

Yes. WZC is Walter Zeppieri, CSLB Lic. #749409, a Class B General Building contractor licensed in California since 1998. The license is current and active, and anyone can look it up on the Contractors State License Board website.

What happens after I call?

Walter talks the job through with you, then comes out to walk the house and check what's behind the walls you want to open. The scope and price go in writing before work starts. When the work is done, you walk the finished rooms together.

What areas do you work in?

WZC is based in Beverly Hills. Call Walter with your address and he'll tell you whether your house is in his area.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. WZC pulls the building permits for your job and books the city inspections.

Do you build ADUs?

Yes. WZC builds accessory dwelling units, both new backyard units and garage conversions.

How much deposit do I pay up front?

California law limits the down payment on a home improvement contract to $1,000 or 10 percent of the contract price, whichever is less. That rule is in Business and Professions Code section 7159.5.

Who will I deal with during the job?

Walter. He owns WZC and holds the license, so he's the person you call from the first visit to the final walk-through.
