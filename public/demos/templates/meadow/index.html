Questions people often ask

How quickly can you come out?

Most weeks we can visit within a few days. Call or send a message and we will offer you the first times that work.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. The first chat and the written quote are free, and there is no obligation to go ahead.

What areas do you cover?

We work across Your Town and the nearby neighbourhoods. If you are a little further out, just ask.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. If something we did is not right, we come back and put it right at no extra cost.

How do I pay?

You pay once the job is done and you are happy. We accept card, bank transfer and most other common methods.
