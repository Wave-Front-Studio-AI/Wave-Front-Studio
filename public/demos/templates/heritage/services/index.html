Careful plans, clear drawings and a written scope agreed before any work begins.

Common questions

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The consultation and written quote cost nothing, and you are under no obligation.

We do. If anything we have done is not right, we will come back and put it right.

A modest deposit secures your date, with the balance due once the work is complete and you are satisfied.

Always. Call the office during opening hours and you will speak with a member of our team.
