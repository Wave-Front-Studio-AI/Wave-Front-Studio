About us

A local name you can trust

A family style business built on good manners, good work and long relationships with the people we serve.

A note from the owner

When I started this business, I made myself a simple promise: treat every customer the way I would want my own family treated. That means honest advice, fair prices and work that lasts.

Many of our customers have been with us for a long time. Some of them now call us about their children's homes and businesses. That trust is the thing I am proudest of, and we work hard every day to keep it.

With thanks, the owner

Our principles

I

Keep our word

Dates, prices and promises are kept. When something changes, you hear it from us first.

II

Take our time

Good work cannot be rushed. We plan carefully so the job is done right.

III

Look after people

Customers, neighbours and our own team. Courtesy costs nothing and matters a great deal.

How we got here

  1. The beginning

    Heritage Co. began with a single phone number, a handful of neighbours and a promise to do every job properly.

  2. Word spreads

    Customers told their friends and family. Most of our work still comes from people who know someone we have helped.

  3. A growing team

    We brought on people who share our standards, and trained them the way we were trained: slowly and carefully.

  4. Today

    The same values, a few more faces, and the same direct line to the people doing your work.
