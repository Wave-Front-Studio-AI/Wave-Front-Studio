Welcome

For as long as we have been in business, our approach has stayed the same. We turn up when we say we will, we explain things plainly, and we stand behind every job with our name. It is an old fashioned way of working, and our customers tell us that is exactly why they come back.

What we stand for

I

Integrity

Fair prices agreed in writing. If we would not do it in our own home, we will not suggest it for yours.

II

Craftsmanship

Skilled hands and patient work. We take the time to do things right the first time.

III

Courtesy

Respect for your time, your property and your questions, from the first call to the final visit.

Our story

  1. The beginning

    Customers told their friends and family. Most of our work still comes from people who know someone we have helped.
