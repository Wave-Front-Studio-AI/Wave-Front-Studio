Small by choice, careful by nature.

Linen Co. is a small team that believes good service should feel calm, clear and personal.

Our story

Linen Co. began with a simple idea: that people deserve service which feels as considered as the work itself. Too often, clients are hurried, left waiting for answers, or handed a price that changes halfway through.

We chose a different pace. We take on fewer projects so we can give each one our full attention. We explain things plainly, put every price in writing and treat every space with respect.

Many of our clients come to us through friends and neighbours. We think that says more than anything we could write here.

What we value

Patience
We never rush a decision. Good choices take a little time.
Clarity
Plain words and written prices, so you always know where things stand.
Respect
For your time, your home or workplace, and your budget.
Care
The small finishing details are where we like to linger.

The people behind the work

A close team of people who enjoy what they do and take pride in doing it properly. You will meet the same faces from beginning to end.
