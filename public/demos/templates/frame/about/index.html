About

Small on purpose.

Frame Co. is a local business run by people who like doing things properly. We take on fewer jobs so each one gets our full attention.

Our story

It started with a few jobs for neighbours. They told their friends, and the work grew from there.

We never wanted to be the biggest. We wanted to be the business people trust with their home or their shop, and the one they recommend without thinking twice.

That still shapes everything: who answers the phone, how we price, and how we leave a space when we are done.
