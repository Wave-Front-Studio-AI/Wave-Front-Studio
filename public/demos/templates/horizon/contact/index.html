Questions, answered

How does the first consultation work?

We meet in person or by phone, listen to what you need and talk through options. It is free and there is no obligation.

How long until I receive a plan?

Usually within a few working days of our first conversation. Larger projects may take a little longer, and we will tell you up front.

Are your prices fixed?

Yes. Your written plan includes a clear price. If anything changes, we agree it with you before going ahead.

Do you offer ongoing support?

We do. Our maintenance plans include scheduled visits and priority booking whenever you need us.
