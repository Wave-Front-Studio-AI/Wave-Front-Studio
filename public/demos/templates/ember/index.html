Good questions

How soon can you start?

Most visits happen the same week you call. Start dates depend on the size of the job, and we give you a firm date with your quote.

Yes. The visit and the written quote cost nothing, and there is no pressure to go ahead.

Do you use subcontractors?

Our own team does the work. If a job ever needs a specialist, we tell you first and stay responsible for the result.
