Ember Co. started with one person, one van and a simple rule: do the job the way you would want it done in your own place.

We grew by word of mouth. Every job is a chance to earn the next one.

Our customers are our neighbours. We see them at the shops and at the school gate, so cutting corners was never an option. That habit stuck as we grew.

Today we are a close team who know each other's work. You get the same faces from the first visit to the final walk through, and a direct number if you need us later.
