Services.

Six services, one standard

Each service follows the same method: listen, plan in writing, do the work properly and stand behind it.

01

Consultations

Every project starts with a conversation. We listen, look at the space, ask the right questions and tell you plainly what we would do and why.

  • First visit at no cost
  • Clear written notes
  • Honest recommendation
02

Planning and design

We turn what we heard into a plan you can read at a glance: the steps, the options, the timeline and one fixed price.

  • Options compared
  • Fixed written quote
  • Agreed timeline
03

Installation and setup

Our team works to the agreed plan, keeps the site clean and keeps you informed. When we leave, everything is tested and ready.

  • Protected workspace
  • Daily progress updates
  • Final walkthrough
04

Repairs

We diagnose before we quote. You get a short explanation of the problem, the fix we recommend and what it will cost.

  • Root cause found
  • Quality materials
  • Work guaranteed
05

Maintenance plans

Choose a visit schedule that suits you. We book the visits, send reminders and keep a simple record of every check.

  • Scheduled visits
  • Reminders sent for you
  • Service record kept
06

Custom projects

Some jobs do not fit a standard list. We plan them from first principles with you, step by step, and price them clearly.

  • Designed from scratch
  • One contact throughout
  • Fixed scope and price
02

How quotes work

Pricing

Is the first visit free?
Yes. The first consultation and the written quote cost nothing and carry no obligation.
Will the price change?
No. The price you sign is the price you pay. If you ask for extra work, we quote it separately first.
When do I pay?
On completion, once you have walked through the finished work with us and you are satisfied.
Is the work guaranteed?
Yes. If something we did is not right, we come back and fix it at no cost.

Ask for a quote.

Describe the job in a few lines. We reply with questions, a visit time and next steps.
