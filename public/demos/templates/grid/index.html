02

Approach

We believe good work is mostly good decisions made early. So we listen first, write everything down and only then pick up the tools. It is slower on day one and faster every day after.

In writing

Scope, price and timeline are agreed on paper before work starts. If something changes, you see it in writing first.

In person

The person you meet at the first visit stays with your job until the end. One name, one number, one plan.

03

Process

  1. Week one

    Listen

    A first visit to understand the job, the space and what matters to you.

  2. Before work

    Plan

    A written scope, a fixed price and a timeline you sign off.
