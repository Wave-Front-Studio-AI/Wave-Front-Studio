Straight answers

Is this really free?

Yes. There is no payment step. Some people read their results and decide they would rather we handle it. That is optional, and a separate decision.

No. We don’t guarantee rankings, leads or revenue, and be careful with anyone who does. What you get is a clear picture of where you stand and what to fix first.

No. We follow up by email, with a short follow-up or two. If you tick the box we will text you as well. Nobody phones you unless you ask us to.
