What we check

Four things decide whether a visitor calls.

Someone opens your site on a phone and on a laptop, the way your customers do, and writes down what goes wrong.

01

Speed

How long someone waits before anything appears, and exactly what is making them wait.

Often found: pages taking five seconds or more to show anything on a phone.
02

Mobile

What your page really looks like on a phone, where most of your visitors actually are.

Often found: buttons too small to tap, and text you have to pinch to read.
03

Google

Whether you show up for what people near you are searching for right now.

Often found: no city or service named on the page, so Google cannot tell where you work.
04

Enquiries

The exact points where a visitor decides not to call, and what to change first.

Often found: a phone number buried at the bottom and a contact form nobody answers.
