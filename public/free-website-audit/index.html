Before you ask

Questions people have first

Why would you do this for free?

Because a fair number of owners who read the report decide they would rather we did the work than do it themselves. Plenty don't, and that is a fair trade for us either way.

Will I get chased with phone calls?

You will not be hassled. The report comes by email first. If we spot something urgent, or you asked us to look at something specific, we may call once to talk it through. Tell us you would rather keep it to email and that is exactly what we will do.

My site is old and I already know it's bad.

That is the most useful case. The report tells you which problem is actually costing you enquiries, so you fix that one first instead of rebuilding everything.

Do you only work with certain industries?

No. Most of the businesses we audit are local service companies - trades, clinics, restaurants, professional services - but any live site is welcome.
